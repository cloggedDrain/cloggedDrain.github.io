Connect OwnTracks to Venom

First: in OwnTracks, open Settings (the i at top left) > Remote Control and turn on Allow external configuration. Then come back here.

Your phone makes a private channel name and an encryption key, seals them so only the Venom box can open them, and gives the same settings to OwnTracks. Your locations are end-to-end encrypted. The relay (ntfy.sh) and this site can't read them.
